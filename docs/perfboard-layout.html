How to Build This

Step 1 — Bus Rails (solder side)

  • Flip board over (solder side up).
  • Right bus strip: solder a continuous bare wire across all 16 pads. This is your GND rail.
  • Left bus strip: same thing — continuous bare wire across all 16 pads. Also GND.
  • Front cross: bare wire along Row 1, from left bus to right bus. Solder at both ends and at C12/R1 (Lock GND tap).
  • Back cross: bare wire along Row 18, from left bus to right bus. Solder at both ends, at C13/R18 (RGB GND tap), and C18/R18 (Recal GND tap).
  • GND feed: short wire from C15/R15 (Pro Micro GND pin) to right bus strip.
  • You now have a GND frame around the entire perimeter. Every GND terminal is 1-2 holes from it.

Step 2 — VCC + Signal Wires (solder side)

  • Solder the two VCC wires (traces 13-14 in the table). Only J1 and J2 pot VCC need power.
  • Solder signal wires (traces 1-12). Each is a piece of insulated wire from one pad to another.
  • For bends: solder at the turn point, strip insulation only where wire touches the pad.
  • Where two wires cross, one passes over the other's pad — insulation prevents shorts.

Step 3 — Components (component side)

  • Flip board back (component side up).
  • Solder two 12-pin female headers at C9 and C15, rows 5-16.
  • Solder R1 (220Ω) leads into C7/R8 and C5/R8.
  • Solder R2 (220Ω) leads into C19/R7 and C21/R7.
  • Plug Pro Micro into sockets (USB toward back/R16).
  • Solder external wires from joysticks/LEDs/toggle to terminal pads.

Clamshell Wire Management

What's Where

  • Top shell: 2 joysticks, 3 LEDs, toggle switch (wires go down to perfboard)
  • Bottom shell: Perfboard + Pro Micro, recal button, USB-C breakout
  • ~16 wires cross the gap. Leave ~75 mm service loop.
  • USB-C: short cable from panel breakout to Pro Micro's own port.

Bundling

  • J1 bundle (4 wires): drop through left gap between J1 base and center
  • J2 bundle (4 wires): drop through right gap
  • Center bundle (8 wires): LEDs + toggle, through center gap
  • Label each bundle with heat-shrink or tape: J1, J2, LED, LOCK
  • Strain relief: hot glue or cable tie at board edge before terminal pads
